title: "Import Zoom meetings"
description: "Connect Zoom to import cloud recording transcripts and summaries, manage sync, and remove access."

Connect Zoom to bring available meeting transcripts and summaries into your Shots Founder Hub notes. The connection reads meeting content; it does not change your Zoom meetings or recordings.

<Note>
  The Zoom connection is currently limited to approved test accounts while external access awaits Zoom review. If Zoom does not allow you to authorize Shots Founder Hub, use [file import](/import-recordings#import-a-transcript) instead.
</Note>

## Before you connect

- [Install Shots Founder Hub](/desktop-installation) and sign in to your Shots Founder Hub account.
- Use the Zoom account that owns the cloud recordings you want to import.
- Your Zoom plan, account permissions, and meeting settings must make cloud recordings and transcripts or meeting summaries available. Shots Founder Hub cannot recover content that Zoom did not generate or no longer retains.
- If your organization requires app approval, ask your Zoom administrator to approve Shots Founder Hub. Administrator approval does not override the current external-access restriction.

The importer checks roughly the past six months of cloud recordings. It imports available transcript text, summaries, action items, meeting titles, and dates. It does not copy audio or video. Meetings without a cloud recording are not discovered by this connection, even if they have a summary elsewhere in Zoom.

## Connect Zoom

1. Open **Settings → Imports** in Shots Founder Hub. You can also connect during the **Bring your meeting history** onboarding step.
2. Find **Zoom** and select **Connect**. Sign in to Shots Founder Hub if prompted.
3. In the browser, sign in to the intended Zoom account and review the requested permissions.
4. Authorize Shots Founder Hub and return to the desktop app. Keep Shots Founder Hub open while the import completes.
5. Review the import result and open the imported meeting notes.

The permissions let Shots Founder Hub identify your connected Zoom account, list your cloud recordings and retrieve available transcripts, and read meeting summaries. They do not let Shots Founder Hub edit meetings or recordings.

## Import new meetings

While connected, Shots Founder Hub checks for new meetings while the desktop app is running. Open **Settings → Imports → Zoom** and select **Sync now** to request a check yourself.

Previously imported meeting IDs are skipped. Sync does not continuously replace existing Shots Founder Hub notes with later edits from Zoom. See [Import meeting history](/imports#understand-the-result) for the import result categories.

## Disconnect and remove access

1. Open **Settings → Imports** in Shots Founder Hub.
2. On the **Zoom** row, select **Disconnect** and complete the browser flow if prompted.
3. Return to Shots Founder Hub and confirm that Zoom offers **Connect** again.

To revoke access directly in Zoom:

1. Sign in to the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us/) with the connected account.
2. Open **My Library** and find **Shots Founder Hub**.
3. Under **Actions**, open the **More** menu, select **Remove**, and confirm the removal.

See Zoom's [app removal instructions](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0062865) if your account's controls differ.

Disconnecting stops future imports. It does not delete notes already imported into Shots Founder Hub or recordings in Zoom. Delete unwanted imported notes separately in Shots Founder Hub. To reconnect, repeat the connection steps above.

## Troubleshooting

**Zoom is missing from Imports:** Shots Founder Hub detects installed meeting apps. Open or install the Zoom desktop app, restart Shots Founder Hub, and check again.

**Authorization is blocked:** Check the test-account restriction above and your organization's app-approval requirements. Make sure you are signing in to the intended Zoom account.

**Setup could not start:** Check your internet connection, sign in to Shots Founder Hub, and try **Connect** again. If the error persists, contact support with the exact error and your Shots Founder Hub version.

**No meetings were imported:** Confirm that the connected account has accessible cloud recordings within the import window. Local Zoom recordings and meetings without cloud recordings are not included. If Zoom has no transcript or summary for a recording, Shots Founder Hub skips it. You can [import an exported transcript or audio file](/import-recordings) instead.

**A new meeting is missing:** Wait for Zoom to finish processing the recording and its transcript or summary, then select **Sync now**. Keep Shots Founder Hub open during the import.

**The connection expired or was removed:** Disconnect the old connection in Shots Founder Hub, then select **Connect** to authorize again.
