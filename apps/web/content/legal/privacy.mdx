## 2. Your local record

The desktop application stores its core notes, transcripts, and meeting metadata locally in SQLite. Recordings and attachments are stored as local files. You can edit, export, or remove this content using the app and your device’s controls.

Local processing requires supported local transcription and language models to be installed and selected. Choosing a hosted model, sharing a note, or enabling another connected feature can send content off your device. Local-first storage does not mean every feature operates offline.

## 4. Recording, transcription, and AI

Recording can capture other people’s voices and personal information. Provide the required notice and obtain the required permission before recording or sharing a conversation.

- **Local models:** supported local models process their requests on your device.
- **Your own providers:** when you supply an API key or endpoint, the content needed for the request is sent to that provider under its terms and privacy practices.
- **Hosted features:** when enabled, hosted transcription receives audio, and hosted summaries or chat receive the relevant text and instructions. Search-enabled AI features can also send search queries to a search provider.

Review the selected provider, the context included in a request, and its applicable terms before sending sensitive information. The operating arrangements and data-use commitments for Playbook-managed hosted AI must be confirmed before this notice takes effect.

## 5. Sync, sharing, and remote access

These features have different data paths:

- **Encrypted sync:** the app includes a sync path that encrypts supported content before upload. Recovery-key handling and the configured service determine access and recovery. Keep your recovery information safe.
- **Shared notes:** sharing can create a server-readable copy so recipients can open content in a browser. Share only content you are authorized to disclose.
- **Cloud API and connectors:** enabling remote access can upload a separate server-readable copy of meeting content for authorized clients. This is separate from encrypted sync.

Playbook operates the cloud-service accounts supporting these features. The deployed hosting, processors, and retention and deletion arrangements must still be documented before this notice takes effect. Services you connect directly remain subject to their providers’ terms and notices.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Local notes and transcripts remain on your device until you remove them. The desktop audio-retention implementation defaults to **Forever** when no explicit or legacy setting overrides it. Available audio settings are **don't save**, **1 day**, **3 days**, **7 days**, **30 days**, and **Forever**. These settings govern local audio, not all hosted data. Deleting an account does not itself remove local copies, exports, or copies held by recipients.

**Uploaded transcription audio:** the callback implementation attempts to delete the Supabase audio object after a terminal transcription callback. Lookup or deletion failures are logged; this is best-effort cleanup, not a guarantee of immediate removal or a fixed retention period.

**Playbook-confirmed retention and deletion practice:** Playbook states that hosted data is retained indefinitely until the user deletes it or requests deletion. Deletion jobs are enabled and monitored, and deletion requests are processed the same day. No formal completion deadline is specified. Same-day processing means handling or initiating the request; it is not a promise that every hosted, provider-held, backup, or log copy is erased that day. These practices are user-confirmed operating facts, not independently observed job results.

**Account-deletion implementation:** the API accepts deletion asynchronously with a pending response. The durable cleanup implementation includes storage removal, Stripe customer deletion, encrypted-sync cleanup, and authentication-account deletion. Loops contact deletion is attempted separately on a best-effort basis; this is a repository path, not a statement that Loops is an active provider. Acceptance does not establish that all copies or provider-held records have been deleted.

The attachment-backup deletion calculation includes an initial safety horizon of **24 hours and 5 minutes**, which can be extended by backup or other cleanup dependencies. This is a minimum waiting constraint in the implementation, **not a completion deadline or deletion SLA**. Same-day request processing must not be described as same-day completion of this cleanup. The core API template enables the cleanup worker. Playbook confirms that deletion jobs are enabled and monitored; worker configuration and successful execution have not been independently verified.

**Workspace retention:** an optional, capability-gated `retention_days` policy applies to shared notes and their snapshots. It is not a retention policy for every local record, cloud record, log, or backup.

For connected features, use the relevant account, sharing, integration, and provider controls to disconnect access or request deletion. The stated indefinite-retention policy does not establish backup erasure timing, log expiry, provider-side prompt or response retention, retained billing records, or legal exceptions; these remain unspecified. No fixed completion deadline is promised in this draft.
